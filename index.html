Our Journey

Our Story

Some of the most beautiful stories begin when we least expect them. Ours began with a simple conversation.

What started as two strangers connecting online soon turned into endless conversations, laughter, and a connection that felt special from the very beginning. There was something comforting about being ourselves, as though we'd known each other for years.

From long drives and heartfelt conversations to travelling across the world, exploring new places, and singing along to bollywood songs on the Brooklyn Bridge, every little adventure brought us closer and gave us memories to cherish.

As the pampered youngest children in our families, we found someone who understood our quirks, our values, and the love we share for our families. Along the way, we've learned that love isn't about always agreeing, but about listening, growing, having the difficult conversations, and choosing each other through it all.

Looking back, we believe God was quietly writing our story, bringing two hearts together in His perfect time.

What began with a simple hello is now becoming our forever.
